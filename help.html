👤 Client client

Clients use the portal to follow their jobs, upload requested documents, and sign off completed work.

What you can do

  • My Jobs — see each of your jobs and its current status in plain language (e.g. "Waiting for your documents", "In review", "Ready to sign").
  • Upload documents — when the firm requests a document, open the job and upload it directly (PDF, image, or Office file).
  • Review & sign — when a job is ready, a Review & Sign banner appears. Sign to confirm and let the firm lodge.
  • Notifications — the bell in the top bar tells you when a document is requested or your job status changes. Clicking a notification opens the relevant job.

What you can't see

Clients never see the firm's internal stages, other clients, or staff dashboards — only your own jobs and the simplified status.

🗂️ Reception reception

Reception is the front door: creating and maintaining client records, and opening new jobs.

What you can do

  • Clients & Entities — add new clients, edit their details, and add entities (individuals, companies, trusts). Each client's email is their portal login, so it must be unique.
  • Create jobs — use + Job on an entity to open a new job, choosing the Accountant and Supervisor from the dropdowns.
  • Reassign staff — open a job and use Reassign staff to change who is assigned.
  • Dashboard — monitor all active jobs and their status.

Good to know

Only users with the accountant role appear in the Accountant dropdown, and only supervisor users appear in the Supervisor dropdown.

🧮 Accountant accountant

Accountants do the hands-on work of preparing each job and requesting what they need from the client.

What you can do

  • See your jobs — the dashboard shows only jobs assigned to you.
  • Request documents — on a job, add a document request with a description and due date. This automatically flags the job as Action Required for the client and clears when everything is received.
  • Upload & manage documents — attach working files to the job.
  • Move the job forward — change the stage as you progress; send it to Supervisor review when ready.

Restrictions

Accountants can only open jobs assigned to them, and cannot create or reassign jobs.

✅ Supervisor supervisor

Supervisors quality-check finished work before it goes to the client for signing.

What you can do

  • Review Queue — a dedicated menu showing every job awaiting review, with a live count badge in the nav.
  • Approve — send the job on to the signature stage.
  • Return to accountant — send it back with a required reason; the assigned accountant is notified.
  • Reassign staff — like reception, supervisors can change assignments.

⚙️ Administrator administrator

Administrators manage the system itself — people, templates, and oversight.

What you can do

  • Admin → Users — create staff and client accounts, set roles, and activate/deactivate users.
  • Templates — edit the notification/email templates used across the hub.
  • Audit — view the activity trail.
  • Full visibility of all clients and jobs on the dashboard.

Not included

The Review Queue is reserved for supervisors, and administrators are not selectable as an accountant or supervisor on a job — assign a dedicated role for those.

🔄 The job workflow

Every job moves through the same stages. Clients see a simplified status; staff see the detailed internal stage.

  1. Job created — Reception/Admin opens a job for an entity and assigns staff.
  2. Waiting for documents — the accountant requests documents; the client uploads them (job shows Action Required).
  3. Documents received → Processing — the accountant prepares the return.
  4. Supervisor review — the supervisor approves or returns with a reason.
  5. Awaiting signature — the client reviews and signs in the portal.
  6. Ready for lodgement → Lodged → Completed — the firm lodges and closes the job.

Two flags can apply at any time: On hold (paused) and Action required (client has outstanding requests).

🔔 Notifications

The bell in the top bar works for everyone. It shows an unread count, and clicking a notification opens the related job (staff → job page; client → portal job).

  • Accountants and supervisors are notified when they're assigned a job.
  • Clients are notified when a document is requested or their job status changes.
  • Accountants are notified when a supervisor returns their job.
  • Use Clear all in the bell panel to dismiss notifications.

Every notification is recorded in the system and can be resent. Real email delivery is enabled once SMTP is configured.

❓ FAQ

Why can't I see any jobs?

Accountants only see jobs assigned to them, and clients only see jobs tied to their email. If a client's login email doesn't match a client record, they'll see nothing — check the email in Clients & Entities.

Why isn't a colleague showing in the assign dropdown?

Only users with the exact matching role appear: accountants in the Accountant list, supervisors in the Supervisor list. Set their role in Admin → Users.

Who can create or reassign jobs?

Reception, Supervisor, and Administrator. Accountants cannot.

A client didn't get a notification — why?

Notifications route to the client's email on their client record. Make sure the client record's email matches the person you expect to receive it.
