Privacy Policy

How Syraxx protects your information

Overview

Syraxx ("we", "us") is the secure client portal operated by Elite Tax & Wealth Advisory. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information we collect

We collect the contact and tax-related information you provide (such as your name, email, phone, address, and documents you upload) so that we can prepare and lodge your tax work.

How we use your information

Your information is used solely to deliver accounting and tax services to you, to communicate with you about your jobs, and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

Security

Access to your account is protected by a password and mandatory two-factor authentication. Sensitive details such as your Tax File Number and bank account are never displayed in the portal.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with the Australian Taxation Office and other parties where required to deliver our services or comply with the law.

Contact

If you have any questions about your privacy, please contact Elite Tax & Wealth Advisory.
