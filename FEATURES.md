# Kaiizen Accounting Platform — Complete Feature Guide

A plain-language overview of everything the platform can do, organised by area.

---

## 🔐 Secure Sign-In & Accounts
- **Email & password login** for all users.
- **Two-factor authentication (2FA)** for extra security — staff and clients can turn on an authenticator app or email code.
- **Sign in with Google** — one-click login using a Google account.
- **Sign in with Facebook** — one-click login using a Facebook account.
- **Self-service password reset** — users can request a reset link by email if they forget their password.
- **Role-based access** — five role types (administrator, supervisor, accountant/offshore staff, reception, and client), each seeing only what's relevant to them.

---

## 👥 Client & Entity Management
- Maintain a full **client list** with contact details.
- Record multiple **entities** per client (individuals, companies, trusts, SMSFs, etc.).
- Add and edit client and entity records from one place.

---

## 📁 Job Workflow (the heart of the platform)
- **Create jobs** for any client/entity, choosing job type, deadline, priority, and assigned staff.
- **Pipeline stages** — every job moves through clear stages (e.g. in progress → supervisor review → awaiting signature → ready to lodge → lodged → completed).
- **Work checklists** tailored to each job type; required steps must be ticked before a job can go to supervisor review, protecting quality.
- **Priority levels** — mark jobs High, Normal, or Low.
- **Assign staff** — allocate an accountant and supervisor to each job.
- **Deadlines** — set and manage due dates (only reception, supervisors, and administrators can change them).
- **Status flags** — mark jobs with states such as on-hold or waiting-on-client.
- **Internal notes** — staff can add, view, and remove private notes on each job.

---

## 🔁 Recurring Jobs
- Set up work that repeats on a **monthly, quarterly, or annual** cycle.
- The platform **automatically creates the next job ahead of its deadline**, ready for the team.
- The **financial year updates automatically** each cycle.
- A dedicated screen lets managers **view, pause, resume, delete, or run** schedules on demand.

---

## ✅ Supervisor Review Queue
- Supervisors get a dedicated **review queue** with a live count badge.
- For each job they can **approve**, **return for changes**, or **request more information**.
- The checklist progress is visible so supervisors know a job is complete before approving.

---

## 📄 Document Management
- **Upload documents** against any job.
- **Download documents** securely.
- **Document requests** — staff can request specific paperwork from clients and track what's still outstanding.
- Documents are organised by **category**.

---

## 📧 Automatic Email Reminders
Sent automatically, only during business hours, and never duplicated:
- **Clients** are reminded to send outstanding documents (after 3 days, then 7 days).
- **Accountants** are alerted when a job is due tomorrow or overdue.
- **Supervisors** are nudged when a job has been waiting for review too long.

---

## 🔔 Notifications
- An **in-app notification bell** on every page shows recent alerts; click one to jump straight to the relevant job.
- **Email notifications** keep clients and staff informed of important updates.
- Users can mark notifications as read or clear them.

---

## 🌐 Client Portal
A simplified, secure area just for clients:
- **View their jobs** and current status.
- **Upload requested documents** directly.
- **Electronically sign** documents that require a signature.

---

## 📊 Dashboards
- **For staff:** their jobs grouped by deadline — Overdue, Due Today, Due This Week, Upcoming.
- **For management:** a complete overview across every stage, plus a **workload breakdown per team member**.
- Overdue jobs are flagged accurately based on their real due date.

---

## 🧮 Tax Tracker
- A built-in tool for tracking personal and business tax information, with the ability to save, load, and export data.

---

## 🤖 AI Assistant
- A built-in **assistant** users can ask questions to for help while working in the platform.

---

## ⚙️ Administrator Tools
For administrators only:
- **User management** — create, edit, disable, and delete user accounts, and reset passwords.
- **Email templates** — customise the wording of automated emails.
- **Audit log** — a record of key actions for accountability (also visible to supervisors).
- **Google Drive integration** — connect a Google Drive for document storage.
